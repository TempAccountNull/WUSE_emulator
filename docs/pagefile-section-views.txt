Coherent pagefile section views

WindowsCodecs CreateFileMappingForCircularBuffer reserves and releases 192 KiB,

The section syscall now creates distinct views with shared storage, requested
addresses and lengths, per-view protections, and range/protection validation.
Closing a section handle preserves its views. Unmapping one view preserves the
others. The backing is released after the final view and handle are gone.
SEC_RESERVE commitment propagates to overlapping views. Snapshots save and
rebuild view aliases; older snapshots without the extension still load.

Icicle keeps permissions in shared virtual mappings and bytes in shared physical
pages. Shared mappings bypass permission-dependent TLB entries. Snapshot COW
updates all aliases in the active address space. Executable-page tracking and
unmapped-page reclamation continue to use the physical page identity.

Scope: pagefile SEC_COMMIT/SEC_RESERVE read, write and execute views on Icicle.
Write-copy and additional allocation flags explicitly return STATUS_NOT_SUPPORTED.
Other backends need their own coherent shared-mapping implementation. This change
does not claim complete file-backed, cross-process or NtMapViewOfSectionEx support.
